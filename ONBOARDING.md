# New owner onboarding

Ask only for missing information. Read existing evidence first.
Save answers in OWNER.md, never in this public questionnaire.
Keep one canonical personal record. Link supporting source files.

## Identity and contact

Collect legal first and last name, preferred name, application email, and phone with country code.
Ask for current city, state, country, and relocation preferences.
Collect LinkedIn, GitHub, and portfolio URLs if the owner uses them.
Request a street address only when a real form requires it.
Do not ask for passwords, session cookies, passport numbers, or government identity numbers.

## Education and availability

Ask for school, degree, major, graduation month, GPA, and its scale.
Ask for full-time start date and any internship or part-time availability.
Check whether the person returns to school after an internship.
Record exact dates separately from estimates.

## Work authorization

Ask for the country where the person wants to work.
Record their own current status and documented work authorization.
Record authorization start and end dates when known.
Ask separately about current authorization, future sponsorship, and unrestricted authorization.
Record exact question wording, checked answer, source, and applicable dates.
Do not infer an approved permit from eligibility for a future permit.
A different immigration-support question may need a separate answer.

## Voluntary demographic answers

Ask once for the owner's preferred answers or their choice not to disclose.
Possible categories include race or ethnicity, gender, veteran status, and disability.
Do not infer these answers from a name, photo, nationality, or biography.
Store the exact selected wording and whether it was owner-checked.

## Evidence and search scope

Collect resume source files, projects, publications, and employment records.
For each experience, record dates, actual role, tools, accomplishments, and supported metrics.
Mark incoming employment, coursework, estimates, and unfinished projects explicitly.
Ask for target roles, locations, salary floor and currency, and employment types.
Ask for experience limits, degree constraints, excluded companies, and company application limits.
Use total compensation only if the owner explicitly accepts it as the salary measure.

## Execution authorization

Ask whether the agent may research, prepare, fill without submitting, or submit.
Record the actual instruction, date, scope, daily cap, and exceptions.
Ask separately about email drafts, sending, and LinkedIn messages.
Never transfer another owner's permission into this workspace.
Keep authorization.mode as none until the owner supplies a scope.

## Scheduling and environment

Ask for timezone, preferred time, model, and whether the computer must stay off.
Check the scheduler's actual model and runtime controls.
Record the real task ID only after creation succeeds.
Record browser availability, private repository access, and resume compiler status.
Do not mark setup complete until a trial run writes a handoff.

## Durable lesson format

Record an ID, trigger, observed result, source evidence, scope, correction, and confidence.
Keep uncertain explanations separate from observed facts.
Supersede an old answer explicitly. Do not leave two conflicting active answers.
